# ASRG Conceptual Core

ASRG is an adaptive narrative governance game about ruling through partial knowledge.

The core is intentionally small:

1. The world has an authoritative hidden systemic state.
2. The player never sees raw truth directly.
3. Adaptive systems are represented as nodes connected by pressure-conducting topology.
4. Logs are epistemic objects carrying memory, visibility, and interpretation.
5. Proxy systems translate lived events into structural pressure.
6. Readers expose situated partial knowledge.
7. Advisors and characters interpret through bias.
8. The Presenter converts filtered knowledge into lived narrative perception.
9. The player acts socially through people and story situations, not directly through raw systems.
10. Actions mutate topology, logs, relationships, and pressure.
11. Narrative emerges from systemic evolution under partial visibility.

The first playable question is:

```text
Can hidden systemic pressure produce believable governance drama?
```

Everything else belongs in the layered docs under `docs/`.
