# Core Ontology

ASRG is a game of governance under partial visibility.

The player does not operate a perfect-information state machine. The player rules through rooms, people, rumors, institutions, logs, and biased interpretation.

## Irreducible Model

```text
Hidden systemic state
-> situated readings
-> biased interpretation
-> narrative presentation
-> social action
-> systemic mutation
```

## Ontological Types

- System nodes are adaptive systems that can generate and receive pressure.
- Topology edges describe where pressure can travel.
- Story nodes are selectable story objects: people, places, logs, threats, achievements, and later artifacts or institutions.
- Logs are epistemic memory: they record what happened, who knows it, how visible it is, and how reliable it may be.
- Places are viewpoints over nearby systems, not necessarily systems themselves.
- Characters are story-facing interfaces to institutions, relationships, and pressure.

## Core Rule

```text
The world owns truth.
The reader owns access.
The presenter owns perception.
The player owns attention and action.
```

The Presenter may dramatize, compress, hint, and frame. It must not directly mutate truth or reveal hidden facts without access.
